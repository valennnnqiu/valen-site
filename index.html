About

VALEN is a Toronto DJ who grew up between Shanghai, Melbourne, San Francisco and Toronto. Moving between cultures taught her to read a room fast, and that is how she plays: open format for clubs, brand events and parties, and fun, happy drum & bass that turns songs you already know into DnB remixes. Every night has the same goal, get everyone dancing, especially the ones who swore they wouldn't.
